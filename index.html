About Our School

Abu Huraira Islamiyya is committed to fostering an environment where spiritual growth and educational excellence go hand in hand. Our mission is to guide students to understand their deen, memorize the Noble Quran, and develop excellent character (Akhlaq) that enables them to positively impact the wider world.

Our Islamic & Quranic Programs

Hifdh (Quran Memorization)

A structured and supportive program guiding students through proper Tajweed principles to successfully memorize and preserve the Holy Quran.

Islamic Studies

Comprehensive classes covering basic Fiqh (Jurisprudence), Seerah (Prophetic Biography), Hadith, and Islamic History tailored for various age levels.

Arabic Language

Focusing on foundational reading, writing, and comprehension skills to help students connect deeply with the language of the Quran.
